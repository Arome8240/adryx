## 🎯 Executive Summary

Adryx is a decentralized advertising platform built on Solana that revolutionizes digital advertising through blockchain transparency, automated payments, and smart contract escrow. We eliminate intermediaries, reduce fees, and provide instant settlements for publishers while giving advertisers complete transparency and control over their campaigns.

**The Problem:**
- Traditional ad networks charge 30-50% fees
- Payment delays of 30-90 days for publishers
- Lack of transparency in ad metrics
- High fraud rates and click manipulation
- Centralized control and arbitrary account suspensions

**Our Solution:**
- 5% platform fee for advertisers, 10% for publishers
- Instant on-chain payments via Solana
- Transparent, verifiable metrics on-chain
- Smart contract escrow for campaign funds
- Decentralized, censorship-resistant platform
