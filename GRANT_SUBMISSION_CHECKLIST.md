## 🎬 Create Your Video Demo

### What to Include (2-3 minutes)
1. **Introduction** (15 sec)
   - Your name and project name
   - One-sentence description

2. **Problem Statement** (20 sec)
   - What problem are you solving?
   - Why does it matter?

3. **Product Demo** (60-90 sec)
   - Show the actual product working
   - Walk through key features
   - Demonstrate advertiser flow
   - Demonstrate publisher flow
   - Show the dashboard

4. **Technology** (20 sec)
   - Mention Solana integration
   - Highlight smart contracts
   - Show it's production-ready

5. **Ask** (15 sec)
   - What you need the grant for
   - What you'll accomplish

### Recording Tips
- Use Loom, OBS, or similar screen recording tool
- Record in 1080p minimum
- Use a good microphone
- Show your face (picture-in-picture)
- Keep it under 3 minutes
- Add captions if possible
- Upload to YouTube (unlisted is fine)

## 🎯 Tips for Success

### Do's
✅ Be specific and concrete  
✅ Show, don't just tell  
✅ Demonstrate traction  
✅ Be realistic about timeline  
✅ Show passion and commitment  
✅ Highlight ecosystem benefits  
✅ Provide working demos  
✅ Be responsive to questions  
✅ Follow up appropriately  
✅ Thank reviewers for their time  

### Don'ts
❌ Overpromise and underdeliver  
❌ Use vague or generic language  
❌ Submit incomplete applications  
❌ Ignore feedback or questions  
❌ Be defensive about criticism  
❌ Rush the application  
❌ Copy from other applications  
❌ Exaggerate achievements  
❌ Neglect the budget section  
❌ Forget to proofread

## 📞 After Submission

### Immediate Actions
1. Save confirmation email/number
2. Note submission date
3. Set reminder for follow-up (2 weeks)
4. Continue building and improving
5. Document any new progress

### If Accepted
1. Thank the team
2. Confirm grant details
3. Set up milestone tracking
4. Begin work immediately
5. Provide regular updates
6. Deliver on promises
7. Share results publicly
8. Give credit to Superteam

### If Rejected
1. Ask for feedback
2. Understand the reasons
3. Improve based on feedback
4. Consider reapplying later
5. Explore other funding sources
6. Keep building regardless
