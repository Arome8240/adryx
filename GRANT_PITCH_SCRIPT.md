## Q&A Preparation

### Expected Questions & Answers

**Q: How do you prevent fraud?**
A: Every interaction is verified on-chain. Publishers must sign transactions, and we track wallet addresses. Our smart contracts enforce rules that make fraud economically unviable.

**Q: What's your revenue model?**
A: We charge 5% to advertisers and 10% to publishers on every transaction. As volume grows, so does our revenue. It's sustainable and scales naturally.

**Q: Why not use an existing ad network?**
A: Traditional networks are centralized, opaque, and expensive. We're building infrastructure specifically for Web3, with features impossible on Web2 platforms.

**Q: What's your go-to-market strategy?**
A: Phase 1 targets Solana dApp developers who need monetization. Phase 2 expands to crypto-native publishers. Phase 3 brings in traditional advertisers exploring Web3.

**Q: How will you compete with Google/Facebook?**
A: We're not competing directly. We're serving a different market - Web3 publishers and crypto-native advertisers who value transparency and decentralization. Our lower fees and instant payments are compelling advantages.

**Q: What happens after the grant?**
A: We launch on mainnet, onboard beta users, prove the model works, then raise a seed round to scale globally. The grant gets us to product-market fit.

**Q: Why should Superteam fund you vs other projects?**
A: We have a working product, not just a pitch deck. We're solving a real problem with a clear business model. And we're building infrastructure that benefits the entire Solana ecosystem.

## Presentation Tips

- **Be confident but humble** - You have a great product, but you're asking for help
- **Show passion** - Let your excitement about the project shine through
- **Be specific** - Use concrete numbers and examples
- **Address concerns proactively** - Acknowledge challenges and explain solutions
- **End with a clear ask** - Make it easy for them to say yes
- **Practice timing** - Stay within 3 minutes for the main pitch
- **Prepare for questions** - Have detailed answers ready
- **Bring energy** - This is exciting technology solving real problems
